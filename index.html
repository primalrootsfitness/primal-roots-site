How we train

Start at the feet

Training is private and in person, in a home gym in Bend. We start every session at the foundation and work our way up.

  • Feet first

    Your feet are where every movement starts. We build strength and control there before we load anything heavy.

  • Fascial lines

    Your body works as connected chains, not isolated muscles. We train movements that link them together, through rotation, reach and stride.

  • Kettlebell strength

    No barbell bench, squat or deadlift days. Strength work is built on kettlebells, maces, landmine and cables.
