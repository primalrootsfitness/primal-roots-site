Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Primal Roots Fitness ("Primal Roots," "we," "us") collects, how we use it and how we protect it. It applies to primalroots.fit, our intake forms, our client portal, and text messages and emails we send.

Information we collect

  • Contact information: your name, phone number, email address and mailing address.
  • Intake and health information: answers you give on our intake forms, including your health history, readiness screen, goals, lifestyle and emergency contact.
  • Coaching information: workouts, check-ins, food and habit logs, messages and progress notes from your training.
  • Scheduling and payment records: sessions booked and attended, and payments made. Card payments are handled by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.

How we use it

We use your information only to provide coaching: to screen for safety, build and adjust your program, schedule sessions, send reminders, communicate with you, keep payment records and contact your emergency contact if needed.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with anyone for their marketing purposes. We share information only:

  • with service providers that help us run the business (such as email, calendar, file storage, client portal hosting, payment processing and texting providers), and only so they can provide those services to us;
  • with a healthcare provider or emergency services when needed for your safety, or when you ask us to;
  • when required by law.

Text messages (SMS)

We will not sell, rent or share your phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers that deliver our messages. See our Terms for details on our text messaging program, including how to opt out.

How we protect it

Your forms and records are stored in password-protected business accounts. Your client portal is private to you and your coach. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to the people who need it to coach you.

How long we keep it

We keep client records for as long as you train with us and for a reasonable period afterward for safety, legal and tax purposes.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing or texting us. Some records, such as signed forms and payment records, may need to be kept for legal reasons. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
