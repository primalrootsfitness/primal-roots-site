Training

How we train

Private, in-person sessions in a home gym in Bend. Every session starts at the foundation and works up.

  • Feet first

    We build strength and control in your feet and ankles before loading anything heavy, so force starts in the right place.

  • Fascial lines

    We train movements that link your body together, foot to hip to opposite shoulder, instead of one muscle at a time.

  • Kettlebell strength

    No barbell bench, squat or deadlift days. Strength work is built on kettlebells, maces, landmine and cables.
