Why most training misses

The usual gym approach is built backwards

Most programs start at the top: pick a muscle, grab a machine, add weight. The foundation everything stands on gets skipped.

The usual approach

  • Machines that move for you and isolate one muscle at a time.
  • Feet ignored, stuffed into thick cushioned shoes.
  • Chest day, leg day, the same template for everyone.
  • Heavy barbell lifts before you can move well.
  • A plan printed off and an hour alone on a treadmill.

The Primal Roots way

  • Start at the feet. Strength and control from the ground contact up.
  • Train connected chains. Movements that work your body as one piece through the fascial lines.
  • Kettlebells, maces and landmine. Rotation, reach and stride, the way you move in real life.
  • Coached every session. One-on-one, in person, with me watching every rep.
  • Built for life outside the gym. Work, sports, kids, and staying capable as you age.

Where we start

Feet first, then the lines that connect you

Feet first

Your feet are where every movement starts. Weak, stiff feet push the work up to your knees, hips and back. We build strength and control there before we load anything heavy.

Fascial lines

Your body works as connected chains, not isolated muscles. Connective tissue links your foot to your hip to your opposite shoulder. We train movements that tie those chains together, through rotation, reach and stride.
